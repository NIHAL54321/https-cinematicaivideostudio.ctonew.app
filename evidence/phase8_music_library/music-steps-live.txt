WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE
=====================================================================================
Proved here, in a browser, on the served build: the search's counts line, the genre filter
filling from catalogue values and changing the decision (0 → 2 export-allowed), a preview
playing with currentTime advancing 2.641151 → 6.646286 s, a refusal quoting the server's
reason and writing nothing, and a permitted selection storing exactly 1 MUSIC row.

Still NOT proved, and not claimed: that music is in any delivered file. It is not — mixing is
a later block, and the panel's own `music-no-mix` line says so. The badge is this product's
licence decision on the uploader's stated licence; it is not a clearance and not a licence
verification beyond Audius' own upload metadata.
